## What v121 releases

### Source before representation

Local admission of an exact sealed proof object is the first authoritative step. Global publication follows as an idempotent append beneath that proof. A server response, database row, cache, index, or UI projection can distribute and accelerate access to truth but cannot manufacture it.

### First paint without remount

A settled profile, live player, identity, or Settlement surface does not reload or remount merely to wait for remote representation. Known proof truth paints immediately; later projections hydrate or reconcile around the same exact identity and head.

### Exact media and stronger-field preservation

Caption-only publication retains a real SHA-256 commitment. Media posts preserve exact source bytes through Safari-compatible transport. A partial projection cannot overwrite or erase stronger verified media, ownership, PBI, composite-head, or proof fields.

### Durable hydration and complete identity session

Durable cold heads restore lawful continuity without making local storage or a database the proof root. Once identity proof has been accepted, the full session can proceed from that proof rather than blocking on weaker identity-key or profile projections.

## Why this matters beyond any one institution

The highest credible frame is truth before institutional representation. A government, company, platform, marketplace, database, game, or AI provider may lawfully recognize, regulate, host, and project a proven identity or event without becoming the institution whose record creates that identity or event. Exact carried proof can remain independently verifiable when a representation is delayed, partial, unavailable, or replaced.

This is not immunity from law, a claim of legal sovereignty, or a denial of legitimate institutional authority. It is an architectural separation: proof establishes the technical fact; institutions interpret and act on it within their lawful roles. The result is continuity capable of surviving changes in institutional containers without requiring a person or digital subject to become a new, amnesiac object at every boundary.
