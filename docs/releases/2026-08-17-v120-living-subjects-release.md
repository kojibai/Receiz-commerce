## What v120 releases

### A proof-native subject

`receiz.subject.v1` binds an immutable subject identifier, proof-object identity, subject type, original owner, identity digest, genesis digest, and creation Kai. Ownership, application, device, provider, memory, condition, relationship, inventory, and world changes append successors. Named and unknown namespaces remain exact bytes through transition, reconciliation, export, import, and transfer.

### A complete proof brain

The reasoning path begins at the exact canonical subject head, searches a bounded index, independently resolves the referenced primary proof-object bytes, verifies them, and cites those objects in the resulting context. Indexes, summaries, embeddings, and model memory are navigation aids, not authority. The ninety-six-object value bounds one reasoning window; it does not cap canonical history.

### AI that can propose, but cannot manufacture truth

Subject Twins can speak, stream voice and performance, retrieve proof context, summarize cited memory, and propose intentions. Model output cannot append an event. A world effect requires a typed command, exact-head validation, current capability or mandate verification, deterministic reduction, atomic append, and a report-only receipt. Failed decisions write zero state, including Kai.

### Bounded autonomy and atomic society

Autonomous mandates bind action types, regions, subjects, owners, privacy, battle, value exposure, approval thresholds, rate limits, memory visibility, expiry, provider, safety, application, and tenant. Runtime execution re-verifies the mandate and current heads after acquiring a durable lease. Multi-subject effects advance every participant and the world together or advance none. Factual memory cites admitted events and remains rebuildable.

### Portable minds and bearer transfer

Portable mind artifacts bind exact identity plus subject, history, memory, and brain heads while preserving public/private memory policy and conflicting forks. Bearer transfer binds the exact ownership head, pending custody, recipient acceptance, expiry, cancellation, replay protection, inventory disposition, and prior-owner conversation policy. A successful claim preserves identity and history while atomically revoking the former owner's mandates, private Twin access, queued jobs, and delegated capabilities. The new owner must authorize autonomy again.

## Why this matters beyond any one institution

The highest credible frame is durable continuity that belongs to the subject's proof history rather than the institution currently hosting it. A platform, company, government, marketplace, game, or AI provider can recognize, regulate, and interact with that subject without becoming the sole keeper of its identity and memory. If one container disappears or becomes untrustworthy, exact carried history remains available for independent verification and lawful continuation elsewhere.

That is not a claim of being outside society, beyond law, or immune to governance. It is a separation of technical truth from institutional custody. Governments and institutions remain legitimate actors in law, safety, coordination, and recognition; they no longer have to be the database whose continued existence defines whether the subject remembers who it is. The architecture therefore supports continuity across changing institutions rather than rebellion against institutions.
