# Examples

## Let the creature explore while its owner is absent.

Activate an exact bounded mandate, enqueue an exact-head tick, reverify it at lease time, and admit only a permitted deterministic command.

## The owner revokes a mandate while a tick is queued.

Cancel the queued job immediately and admit zero events.

The owner and worker proof objects, current heads, allowed commands, world/region, Phi and geometry limits, expiry, nonce, and revocation head are all bound into the v122 mandate digest. The worker includes that digest in every plan and execution.
