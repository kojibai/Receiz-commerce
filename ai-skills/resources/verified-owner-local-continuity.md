# Verified Owner Local Continuity

An admitted Identity Seal, Identity Record, or Receiz Key projects the complete
verified account at its exact sealed KKS/history head immediately. Local proof
objects, media, history, cards, packs, wallet/Reserve state, preferences, and
other carried namespaces are permanent append-only local truth.

Closing or reopening the PWA, restarting, deploying, reconnecting, or receiving
global acknowledgement never deletes or hides that truth. Acknowledgement changes
delivery status only.

Hydration is permitted only for independently verified descendants after and
bound to the admitted sealed head, including later transactions, messages from
other actors, Twin activity, and other global appends. State at or before the
sealed head is already known and must never hydrate or be reconstructed.

Infrastructure is distribution and indexing only. It cannot block local access,
display, use, or lawful proof creation for the verified owner. Invalid proof,
unauthorized deltas, predecessor-head conflicts, and operations outside the
admitted capability still fail closed.
